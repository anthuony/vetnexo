Questions

Before you get started.

Is the $2,500 implementation included in the monthly plan?

No. Implementation is a one-time fee, quoted separately from the subscription. The agreed scope includes basic migration, configuration, initial training and go-live support.

How does the pilot credit work?

The $499 pilot fee is credited in full toward the implementation fee if you subscribe within 15 days after the pilot ends, subject to the written pilot agreement.

Can you migrate our existing records?

Basic migration of clients, pets, products and services is part of the proposed implementation scope. Data format, volume and mapping must be reviewed before confirming the scope.

Can I use this demo with real patient data?

No. This public demo uses fictional sample data and is for evaluation only. Production hosting, security review, privacy requirements and data migration must be agreed before real clinic data is entered.

Does VetNexo support multiple locations?

The Multi-Location offer is a proposed package. Availability and consolidated reporting must be confirmed during discovery and documented in the final agreement.
